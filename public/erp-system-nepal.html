Why Nepal businesses need a connected ERP system

A company may use one software for accounting, another for inventory, another for sales tracking, and another spreadsheet for customers or employees. This creates duplicate data, mismatched reports, slow decision-making, and no clear end-to-end view of the business.

Bebasthapan is designed as an advanced ERP system for core business functions: accounting, inventory, sales, clients, invoices, purchases, supplier payments, attendance, and business insights. The goal is to help owners and managers see the complete business from one place.
